# Limit Phase 1 ML assistance to three explainable algorithms

Phase 1 may add ML-assisted analysis, but only with linear regression for near-term temperature direction or value, logistic regression for the probability of an out-of-range reading, and k-means clustering for grouping Pod behavior. The models assist an operator's review and must expose their algorithm and data basis; they must not make automatic stock-safety or disposition decisions.
